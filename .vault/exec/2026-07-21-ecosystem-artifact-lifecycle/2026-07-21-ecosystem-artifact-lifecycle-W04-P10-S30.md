# Amend Layer 3 in place to name the a2a-owned trace and redirect the obligation at the said-versus-did gap

## Scope

- `.vault/adr/2026-07-21-ecosystem-artifact-lifecycle-adr.md`

## Description

<!-- Succinct line-by-line list of steps executed. Use imperative language, mirroring git commit summary lines. -->

- Replace the third layer's transcript clause with the obligation the evidence
  supports, in place, since the record is still proposed.
- State the three findings that force the change and the date they were made.
- Name the trace of record as this project's own stores rather than a provider's
  files.
- Record the ACP operator-home persistence as a residual exposure belonging to
  the confinement trail rather than to this layer.

## Outcome

The layer is REDIRECTED, not struck. Striking it would have discarded a valid
concern along with its wrong instrument: the protected quantity is visibility,
and that survives the disappearance of the artifact named for it. Retaining it
conditionally - "activate if the ephemeral posture ever changes" - was rejected
for the opposite reason, since a clause conditioned on a posture nobody intends
to change is text that can never fire.

The replacement attaches the obligation to stores this project owns: the
checkpoint transcript served with truthful availability, the durable permission
decisions, and the control-actions journal. Provider session files are explicitly
named as NOT that record, on the grounds that they do not exist on the lane that
was assumed to produce them and, on the lanes where they do exist, this project
could not own their retention anyway.

The closing work is restated as action-event capture at the provider seam, under
one retention vocabulary and one owner, bounded - and the amendment says why the
alternative loses: per-provider file salvage would be lane-specific, in a
provider's format, and a second at-rest copy of message text this project already
holds.

## Notes

<!-- Incidents. Data loss. Difficulties; persistent failures. Skipped work. Scaffolds left in code. Failures. -->

Amended in place rather than superseded because the record is still `proposed`;
a superseding record would have been the wrong instrument for correcting a
premise inside a decision nobody has ratified yet.

The amendment deliberately does NOT specify the capture seam. That choice is a
separate Step gated on an inventory that is still running, and writing a design
into the decision record ahead of its evidence is the failure this whole wave
exists to correct.
