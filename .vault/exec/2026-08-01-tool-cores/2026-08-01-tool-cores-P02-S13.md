# Land the web-locator extractor in the research producer so a real retrieval becomes a typed locator, and decide in the same change whether the producer normalises a malformed locator or the branch refuses it

## Description

- Extract typed web locators from a researcher turn's prose in a dedicated
  module, stamping one retrieval time per turn.
- Return locators in first-seen order with duplicate URLs collapsed, so a
  replayed turn yields byte-identical evidence.
- Wire the extractor into the research producer, which previously returned an
  empty locator list unconditionally.
- Give the researcher branch a retry policy and drop a guard the change made
  untriggerable.

## Outcome

The typed channel has a production emitter, so the contract admits a shape
something now produces and the submit refusal can fire in a real run.

The load-bearing decision was taken as neither of the two options the row
offered, and the third is better than both. The producer neither normalises a
malformed locator nor lets the branch refuse it: it emits only locators that
already satisfy the branch-side validation, and omits anything that could not be
made to satisfy it rather than repairing it by guesswork. The docstring says so
in those terms.

That resolves both findings this Step was created from, and resolves them by
construction rather than by handling. The dead channel now has an emitter. The
abort path - branch validation raising out of a node with no retry policy -
becomes unreachable, because a malformed locator never reaches the validator to
raise on. The Step also gave the branch a retry policy and removed a guard that
became untriggerable once the producer could no longer emit a violation, so the
node no longer carries a defence against something that cannot happen.

Omission over repair is the right instinct for the same reason the validator
refuses over truncating: only the producer knows which part of a retrieval
carries the claim, and a guessed scheme or a trimmed excerpt is evidence nobody
authored. Dropping a locator that cannot be made conformant loses a citation;
inventing one loses the reader's ability to trust any of them.

## Notes

The scheme-less-host case that a reviewer singled out - the likelier malformation
in practice, and the one that needed naming because prefixing a scheme is a
different operation from truncating an excerpt - is handled by the same rule
rather than by a case: a URL whose host does not survive extraction is omitted,
not guessed at.

The executing agent reached its session limit mid-flight, so this record is
written from the committed tree and the passing acceptance suite rather than its
report. The extractor, its wiring, the retry policy, and the per-malformation
coverage are verified present; what is not verified in its own words is whether
it weighed normalise-at-producer before settling on omission, or arrived at
omission directly.
